Enquiries

Frequent questions.

Not covered here? Call +61 8 8376 4495 and ask.

Does using you cost more than booking direct?

Usually the opposite. We buy tee times and rooms at operator rates, which is how clients end up paying less than the walk-in green fee at places like Denarau. The itinerary and the quote cost you nothing, so it is easy enough to compare.

Where are you based, and do you only do Australia?

We are based in Glenelg, South Australia, and we book more than 25 destinations worldwide. Adelaide, Tasmania and the Mornington Peninsula through to New Zealand, Fiji, Vietnam, South Africa, Scotland, Ireland and the USA.

Will you do a short break, or is it big trips only?

Both. Our shortest packages are three night breaks from AU$769 per person, and our longest run several weeks across multiple countries. Tell us the budget and we will work to it.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Golf Explorer holds Travel Agent Licence TTA 208954 and is an accredited member of the International Association of Golf Tour Operators, Member #2667. Our managing director previously ran IAGTO, so the standard is not something we guess at.

How fast will I get a quote?

We respond to every enquiry the same business day. A full costed itinerary usually follows within a couple of days, depending on how many clubs we need to confirm tee times with. Send the brief and we will tell you exactly when it will land.

What about the people in the group who don't play?

They get their own itinerary. Resort and spa days, wine tours, island trips, whatever suits. Nobody sits in a hotel lobby for four days waiting for the golfers to come in.
